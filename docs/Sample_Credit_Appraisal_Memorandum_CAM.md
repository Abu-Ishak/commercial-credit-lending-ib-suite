## 2. Lead Proposal Deep-Dive: `Meghna Knit Composite Ltd.` (`CAM-2026-001`)
- **Facility Structure:** `BDT 180.0M` 5-Yr Term Loan (`13.50%` quarterly amortizing) + `BDT 120.0M` `CC (Hypo)` Working Capital + `BDT 250.0M` Back-to-Back `LC / LTR` Trade Limit = **`BDT 550.0M` Total Limit**.
- **MPBF Working Capital Justification:** Net Operating Cash Cycle of **`56 days`** (`DIO 48d + DSO 42d - DPO 34d`) yields a working capital gap of `BDT 350.19M`; after deducting mandatory `25%` borrower margin (`BDT 87.55M`), `75% MPBF` supports up to **`BDT 262.64M`** (sanctioned `CC` limit capped prudently at `BDT 120.0M`).
- **Trade Finance Cycle (`Sight LC -> PAD -> 90d LTR`):** On an illustrative `BDT 100M` raw-material LC with `20%` cash margin (`BDT 20.0M`), shipping documents lodge under `PAD`, converting `80%` (`BDT 80.0M`) to a `90-day LTR` maturing at `BDT 82.66M` (incl. `BDT 2.66M` interest) retired strictly from liened export proceeds.
- **Credit Risk & Pricing (`RAROC`):** `PD 1.10%` x `LGD 32.0%` = `0.35% Expected Loss`; Net Spread after opex & EL generates **`36.8%` Pre-Tax `RAROC`** vs. `15.0%` bank hurdle rate (`Base DSCR 2.52x` / `Stress 2.03x`).

## 3. Why `Jamuna Steel Trading Syndicate` (`CAM-2026-003`) Was Declined
- **Bangladesh Bank Quant Floor Trigger:** Quantitative score of **`27.0 / 60` (`45.0%`)** breaches BB's mandatory **50% quantitative floor**, forcing an automatic **`Unacceptable`** override regardless of its `73.0%` qualitative relationship score.
- **Adverse CIB & Stress DSCR Failure:** `CIB` reveals **2 overdue LTRs in `SMA` status** at a peer bank, Leverage is `3.45x`, and Base DSCR (`0.87x`) is below `1.00x`. Declined per zero-tolerance asset quality policy.
