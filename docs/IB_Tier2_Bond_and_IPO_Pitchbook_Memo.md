## 2. DCM Pitch: Basel III Subordinated Tier-2 Floating-Rate Bond (`BDT 5,000M`)
- **Structure:** Non-convertible, unsecured, subordinated Tier-2 bond, **7-Year Tenor** (`20%` annual redemption in Years 3–7; Years 1–2 principal grace).
- **Coupon Pricing:** `6M FDR Benchmark (9.75%) + 275 bps = 12.50%` semi-annual (Floor `11.00%`, Cap `14.50%`).
- **Duration Risk:** Macaulay Duration **`3.80 yrs`**, Modified Duration **`3.57 yrs`**, `DV01 = BDT 1.786M`.
- **Pro-Forma CRAR Impact:** On `BDT 352,000M` Risk-Weighted Assets, Tier-2 issuance lifts total CRAR from **`14.40%` to `15.82%` (`+142 bps`)**, expanding loan growth headroom while preserving CET1 equity.
